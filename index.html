About BlueHarbor

Practical, locally grounded advice for capital decisions.

BlueHarbor Advisory Limited is an investment advisory and corporate finance firm focused on connecting growth-oriented businesses with suitable sources of capital and supporting investors through informed transaction decisions. Based in Kampala, the firm serves enterprises, investors and development-focused institutions seeking practical, locally grounded advice across Uganda and selected East African markets.

Our work combines financial analysis, investment readiness, due diligence and transaction support with an understanding of the operating realities faced by businesses and capital providers. Engagements are scoped to each client's mandate, sector, financing needs and stage of growth.

Who we support

Growth-stage SMEs and established businesses preparing for investment, expansion or strategic financing.

Who we support

Private investors, funds and investment teams requiring disciplined sourcing, screening and transaction support.

Who we support

Development programmes, public-interest organisations and partners supporting enterprise growth and access to finance.

Our advisory services

Three pillars, from preparation to execution.

Three complementary service pillars support enterprises from investment preparation to execution and help investors move from opportunities to informed decisions.

01Investment & Transaction Advisory

We support investors and companies in identifying, evaluating, structuring and advancing transactions, drawing on financial, commercial and execution perspectives. Responsibilities are agreed for each mandate; introductions are distinguished from advisory and execution services.

Learn more about this service

02Development Finance & Programme Advisory

We assist mission-aligned programmes seeking to improve enterprise competitiveness, access to finance and market participation. Support can be provided directly or as part of a consortium, subject to a defined engagement scope.

Learn more about this service

03Corporate Finance & Enterprise Advisory

We help growth-stage businesses translate strategy and operating performance into credible, investable financing propositions. The scope may cover financing plans, business analysis and preparation for lender or investor engagement.

Learn more about this service
