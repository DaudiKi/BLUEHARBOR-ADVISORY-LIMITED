How BlueHarbor Advisory Limited collects, uses and protects information shared through this website.

1. Who we are

BlueHarbor Advisory Limited ("BlueHarbor", "we", "us") is an investment advisory and corporate finance firm based in Ntinda, Kampala, Uganda. This notice explains how we handle personal information submitted through this website, for example via the contact form.

2. Information we collect

  • Contact details you provide (name, organisation, email, phone number).
  • The content of your enquiry, including the nature of your mandate.
  • Standard technical data collected by our hosting provider (e.g. IP address, browser type) for security and analytics purposes.

3. How we use it

We use information you submit solely to respond to your enquiry and, where an engagement proceeds, to deliver the advisory services agreed with you. We do not sell personal information to third parties.

4. Confidentiality

Consistent with our operating principle of integrity, information you share is treated confidentially and is only shared internally, or with external specialists engaged on a mandate, on a need-to-know basis.

5. Your rights

You may ask us at any time to access, correct or delete personal information we hold about you by emailing info@blueharboradvisory.com.

6. Contact

Questions about this notice can be sent to info@blueharboradvisory.com or to our office in Ntinda, Kampala, Uganda.
