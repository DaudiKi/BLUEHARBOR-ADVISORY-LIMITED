Our advisory services

Three pillars, from preparation to execution.

Three complementary service pillars support enterprises from investment preparation to execution and help investors move from opportunities to informed decisions.

We help growth-stage businesses translate strategy and operating performance into credible, investable financing propositions. The scope may cover financing plans, business analysis and preparation for lender or investor engagement.

Typical deliverables

  • Investment readiness reviews
  • Financial models and forecasts
  • Business valuations
  • Investment memoranda and pitch materials
  • Capital requirements and financing options
  • Investor mapping

Learn more about this service

We support investors and companies in identifying, evaluating, structuring and advancing transactions, drawing on financial, commercial and execution perspectives. Responsibilities are agreed for each mandate; introductions are distinguished from advisory and execution services.

Typical deliverables

  • Opportunity screening
  • Investment appraisal
  • Due diligence coordination
  • Valuation and return analysis
  • Transaction structuring support
  • Investment committee materials
  • Negotiation and financial-close support
  • Portfolio performance reporting

Learn more about this service

We assist mission-aligned programmes seeking to improve enterprise competitiveness, access to finance and market participation. Support can be provided directly or as part of a consortium, subject to a defined engagement scope.

Typical deliverables

  • Enterprise sourcing and selection
  • Readiness diagnostics
  • SME financial literacy
  • Technical-assistance design
  • Investment facilitation
  • Programme reporting and outcomes tracking

Our engagement approach

A clear course, agreed from the outset.

  2. 1

    Define

    Agree objectives, scope, deliverables, timing and responsibilities.

  3. 2

    Evaluate

    Review business fundamentals, risks, commercial assumptions and funding needs.

  4. 3

    Prepare

    Develop models, investment materials, transaction options and diligence outputs.

  5. 4

    Support

    Assist decisions, stakeholder coordination, execution and post-investment reporting.
